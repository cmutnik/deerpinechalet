Heating

Thermostat

Model: BRAND AND MODEL. It's located in the living room, next to the stairs.

Set the temperature

  1. Check that the mode is Heat.Press MODE until the screen shows HEAT.
  2. Use the arrows to choose a temperature.The setting saves after a few seconds.
  3. Give it time.Turning it all the way up doesn't heat the chalet faster. Pick the temperature you want and let it catch up.
  4. Schedule or hold.Explain whether a schedule is programmed and how to override it, e.g. press HOLD to keep your setting.

Suggested settings

WhenSetting
Home and awake66–70 °F
Sleeping62–65 °F
Out for the day60–62 °F
At checkout60 °F

Please don't turn the heat off in cold weather

Never set it below 55 °F from fall through spring. Pipes can freeze fast in an Adirondack winter.

If it isn't heating

  • Check the mode and setpoint.It should say Heat, and the setting should be above the room temperature.
