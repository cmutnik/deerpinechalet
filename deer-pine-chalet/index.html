Good to know

  • Bring reusable grocery bags.New York bans plastic bags, and many stores charge for each paper bag.
  • Local brochures are in the kitchen drawer.Menus and pamphlets for restaurants and attractions nearby.
  • Go early to the Whiteface Veterans Memorial Highway.It's the easiest big view in the area and it gets busy by late morning.
  • Carry a little cash.Some local spots, like Lance's Place in Au Sable Forks, are cash only.
  • Pack trekking poles if you have them.Trails here are rocky and rooty, and poles help on the downhills.
  • Plattsburgh Airport is about an hour away.Plan your departure with that in mind, and add time in snow.
