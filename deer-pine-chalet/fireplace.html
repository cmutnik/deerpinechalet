Fireplace

Using the fireplace

Type: wood-burning fireplace / wood stove / gas. Firewood is stacked on the porch.

Light a fire

  1. Open the damper.The handle is inside the firebox, above the opening. Look up the chimney or hold a lit match near it to feel the draft pull upward.
  2. Build small.Crumpled newspaper or a fire starter on the bottom, a handful of kindling on top, then two or three small split logs.
  3. Light the paper in a few places.Once the kindling catches, close the screen or doors.
  4. Add wood one or two logs at a time.A small, hot fire throws more heat and less smoke than a packed one.

Burn only the dry firewood provided. Don't burn trash, cardboard, wrapping paper, painted or treated wood, or fresh pine branches.

Putting it out

  1. Stop adding wood and let it burn down.Spread the embers with the poker so they cool faster. Never pour water on the fire.
  2. Leave the damper open until it's completely cold.That usually means the next morning. Closing it early pushes smoke and carbon monoxide into the room.
  3. Handle ashes only when cold.Scoop them into the metal ash bucket by the hearth and set it outside on a non-flammable surface, away from the chalet. Ashes can hold heat for days.

Fire safety

Never leave a fire unattended or burning when everyone is asleep. Keep rugs, blankets and anything flammable at least 3 feet from the hearth, and keep the screen closed while it burns.

If you hear a loud roaring or rumbling from the chimney, or see flames or sparks coming out of the top, you may have a chimney fire. Get everyone out and call 911.

Smoke and carbon monoxide detectors are in the hallway and each bedroom. A fire extinguisher is location.
