Trails

Trails out the door

The neighborhood has its own trail network, and one trailhead is a short walk from the chalet.

Getting to the trailhead

  1. Walk up the road.The chalet is the last one on the road. The trail entrance is about 100 ft further up.
  2. Look for the roundabout.People park there, and the trail starts beside it.
  3. Check the trail map.A map of the neighborhood trails is posted on the chalet, e.g. by the front door.

Lake Trail

The easiest walk in the neighborhood: about a mile, basically level, and literally around the lake at the base of the mountain. Watch for the beaver dams along the shore.

It's a good choice if you want something gentle on the knees or have little ones along.

Trail tips

  • Trekking poles help.Adirondack trails are rocky and rooty, even the short ones.
  • Wear bright colors in the fall.Hunting seasons run through autumn in the Adirondacks. Blaze orange is a smart choice on any wooded trail.
  • Check for ticks.Do a quick check after walks from spring through fall.
  • Don't leave food or trash outside.Black bears live here. Keep garbage inside or in the bear-proof bin until pickup.

More easy walks nearby

The Things to do map includes several short, mostly level outings: the Flume Trail waterfall in Wilmington, the High Falls Gorge boardwalks, Monument Falls on Route 86, and the trails at The Wild Center in Tupper Lake.
